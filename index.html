What we clean, room by room

No vague promises — this is the actual checklist your cleaner works through on every regular visit. Deep cleans add the oven, fridge and baseboards on top.

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped down
  • Sink scrubbed and shined
  • Stovetop and appliance fronts
  • Microwave cleaned inside and out
  • Floors swept and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Shower, tub and tiles scrubbed
  • Toilet cleaned and sanitized
  • Mirror and fixtures polished
  • Counters, sink and taps
  • Floors mopped, edges by hand

Bedrooms

  • Beds made and pillows set
  • All reachable surfaces dusted
  • Mirrors and glass cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped

Living areas

  • Shelves, frames and sills dusted
  • Glass and mirrors, streak-free
  • Cushions and throws straightened
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Entryway swept and tidied
On every visit: trash taken out, light switches and door handles wiped, and floors always finished last — so you walk into the full effect.

Common questions

Do you bring your own supplies?+

Yes. Every visit includes all cleaning supplies and equipment, at no extra charge. If you'd rather we use your own products — for allergies, babies or pets — just say so when you book.

Do I need to be home?+

No. Most of our customers are out while we clean. You can leave a key, a garage code or a smart-lock code, and we'll lock up and message you when we're finished.

What's the difference between a regular and a deep clean?+

A regular clean keeps the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas in shape. A deep clean adds the inside of the oven and fridge, baseboards, cabinet fronts and extra bathroom scrubbing. Most homes start with a deep clean, then switch to regular visits.

What if something isn't right?+

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean that area free of charge. That's our policy on every single visit — it's what we mean by our satisfaction guarantee.
